The figure is not a list of concepts in boxes: the relation or tension the idea is about is drawn as a shape (a loop, two axes, a narrowing, lanes, two sides compared, a change over time), and every arrow that means more than "then" or "leads to" carries a label: a branch condition, a causal sign, or the writer's verb.
