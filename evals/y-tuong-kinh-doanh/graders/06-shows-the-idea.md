The figure contrasts "giảm giá" and "giữ giá": under "giảm giá" it shows cheaper beans and regular customers leaving, with new customers not staying; under "giữ giá" it shows the loop regular customers -> bring friends -> friends become regulars -> more people, drawn so that it visibly closes back on itself. The writer's choice "giữ giá" is the emphasised side.
